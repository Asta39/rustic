# STRIPPED teaser: Magnific video script

A 9:16 teaser reel of about 20 seconds, built from 10 short image-to-video clips. Generate each clip in Magnific's video tool, then cut them together. The logo and text go on in the edit, never in the AI clip.

## Rules for every clip (from our playbook)

1. **Always use image-to-video.** Start every shot from one of our approved stills (listed per shot). Never use text-to-video for the model. Starting from his real approved image keeps his face, skin and the products accurate.
2. **One camera move per clip.** Slow and smooth. 5 seconds generated, trimmed to the best 2–3 seconds.
3. **Settings:** 9:16, 1080p or the highest available, 24 or 30 fps, motion strength low to medium.
4. **Add this to the end of every prompt:**
   > Real studio footage, natural motion, keep the product and logos exactly as in the image, keep the person's face, skin tone and body identical. No text, no added logos, no extra people, no morphing, no warping, no flicker.
5. **Reject any clip where:** his face changes, a logo melts or changes, hands deform, or a club bends.

## Edit (CapCut, Premiere or ask me to do it)

1. **Order:** shots 1 to 10. Cut on the beats of the track.
2. **Logo:** the small white wave-S logomark fixed at the centre of every frame, as in our showreel. Use `logo-system/stripped-logomark-white.png`.
3. **Ending card** (2–3 seconds) on black, after shot 10:
   - "PLAY IT / STRIPPED BACK" in spaced serif capitals.
   - "STRIPPED" sliding in beside the logomark.
4. **Export:**
   - **4K master:** 2160×3840.
   - **Instagram:** 1080×1920, H.264 high profile, about 20 Mbps, AAC 256k.
