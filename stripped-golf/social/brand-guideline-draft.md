# STRIPPED social brand guideline (draft 1)

For Instagram and TikTok, Stage 1 (before stock arrives). Built on the Community Blueprint (29 Oct 2026).

This draft comes from what I already know of the community-led golf brands below. It is not yet a fresh scan of their current feeds; that pass needs your screenshots or a browser on your computer.

## 1. What the community brands do that equipment brands don't

| Brand | What they actually sell | What we take | What we leave |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Malbon Golf** | A lifestyle: golf as culture, art and friendship. Warm film-look campaigns, store-as-clubhouse, collabs, events, "Malbon Mondays" style recurring moments | The warm film grade; store and events as content; people first, product second; recurring moments people wait for | Heavy reliance on collabs and LA celebrity |
| **Hype Golf** | Golf culture media plus apparel: the Hype Golf Championship, crew trips, reviews of sneakers and gear, community rounds | Being the *media* for local golf, not just a shop; events with their own name; crew on camera | Hype-beast resale tone |
| **Eastside Golf** | Golf for people the game left out; the founders' story; Black golfers front and centre | A clear "who it's for" stance; the founders on camera; pride without preaching | US-specific references |
| **Bogey Boys, Metalwood Studio, Students of the Game** | Friend-group golf, humour, design-led merch, short funny clips | Banter and bad shots celebrated; merch that works off course | In-jokes no Nairobi golfer gets |
| **Titleist, TaylorMade** (equipment) | Tour proof, tech, fittings | The Fitting Files format: problem, numbers, fix | Tour-pro polish and spec-sheet posts |

The pattern across all the community brands: **the account is a club you watch, not a shop you browse.** Product shows up worn by people you start to recognise.

## 2. Our story in one line

> Golf in Kenya deserves a proper home, and you're already part of it.

Every post should be one of these:
1. Here's who's in the club (people).
2. Here's the house being built (behind the build).
3. Here's what golf in Kenya looks like right now (culture).
4. Here's the gear the club wears (drops).
5. Here's something that makes your game better (help).

## 3. Who we show

- **Real Nairobi golfers first.** Our model is the face of the brand while we wait for members, but by month two at least half the people posts are real golfers.
- **Ladies golfers in every week's posts.** Not as a special feature.
- **The founders on camera** in Behind the Build. That builds trust faster than any logo.
- **Age 22–40, from clubs and from the street.** Karen members and range first-timers in the same feed.

## 4. Visual rules

**One look, two moods:**

| Mood | Use for | How it looks |
|---|---|---|
| **Studio** (lookbooks, drops) | Gear and drops; the model shots | Cream or warm-grey wall, soft window light, an 85mm lens feel, the Malbon warm grade: creamy whites, matte lifted blacks, fine grain. Square or 4:5 |
| **Course** (people, culture) | Play With Me, Course Notes, range nights | Real daylight on Kenyan courses, the red earth, jacaranda and acacia, phone-shot is fine, the same warm grade lightly. 9:16 |

**Always:**
- People in two of every three posts.
- The real place named: a location tag on everything.
- Product worn or used, not floating.
- The wave-S logomark small, never shouting.

**Never:**
- Other brands' logos on clothes (swoosh, etc.), unless it's a partner post with written terms.
- Stock-photo smiling; staged handshakes.
- Pure-white e-commerce shots in the main grid. Those live on the site.
- Polished "hospitality advert" posts, which our scan showed get little engagement in Kenya.

**Grid rhythm (Instagram):** alternate a people shot, a texture or detail shot, and a place shot, so the grid reads like a magazine.

## 5. Voice

- **Short, warm, a little cheeky, never snobbish.** English first, Sheng where it lands naturally.
- **Talk like a member, not a shop.** "We", "the crew", "see you at the range".
- **Caption shape:** one punchy line, one line of context, one call to action (link, sign-up or event).
- **Tagline:** #PlayItStrippedBack on every post.

Examples:
- *"First tee nerves are free. Everything else ships Friday."*
- *"Muthaiga, 7am. Who's coming?"*
- *"We're building Nairobi a golf house. This is week one."*
