## Appearance

- **Age:** late teens to early twenties.
- **Skin:** very deep brown, even tone, smooth with a natural soft sheen. Never lightened or warmed toward orange.
- **Hair:** short natural black hair in tight coils, about 2 to 3 cm long, slightly rounded on top, natural hairline. No fade, no part.
- **Face:** long oval face, high defined cheekbones, almond-shaped dark brown eyes, broad nose, full lips, clean-shaven, calm and neutral expression.
- **Build:** tall and very slim, narrow shoulders, long neck, long arms and legs, flat torso. Not muscular.
- **Height in frame:** tall and lean; clothes hang loosely unless they are fitted styles.

## Rules for every shot

1. Always attach the face photo plus at least 2 full-body photos as references.
2. Repeat the appearance description in the prompt.
3. Keep the same studio set: seamless warm off-white or light grey backdrop, soft diffused light.
4. No earrings, watches or jewellery unless the product needs it.
5. Every image is square, 1:1 aspect ratio, at 4K.
6. Check every result against the face photo before using it; reject any image where his skin tone, hair or build has changed.
7. Always build from the original reference photos, never from an earlier generated shot, so his face does not drift between shoots. Compare each result side by side with model-ref-1-face.
